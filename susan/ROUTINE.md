# Susan's 25-Week Program

Source of truth for `susan/index.html`. Same exercises as Dave's original (v1) routine, with lighter starting weights and lower reps. Program: **Sunday Nov 8, 2026 → Saturday May 1, 2027** (25 weeks).

Weight steps in the app: kettlebells 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35 · dumbbells 3, 5, 8, 10, 12, 15, 20 (tap the number to type any weight).

## 🔥 WARM-UP (Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday — before lifting)

2 rounds, 30 seconds each:

1. Butt-Kicker Air Jump Rope
2. Alternating Reverse Lunges
3. Jumping Jacks
4. Inchworm into Push-Up
5. Spider Lunge into Down Dog
6. Burpees with Jump

## 🗓️ THE WEEKLY ROUTINE

### SUNDAY – THE BURNER (20 Min AMRAP)

- KB Deadlifts – 12 reps – 15 lbs
- Push-ups – 8 reps (knee push-ups are fine — build to full)
- KB Snatches – 6 reps / arm – 15 lbs
- DB Thrusters – 10 reps – 8 lbs
- Then: 30 Min Elliptical – Zone 2 to Low Zone 3 (or StairMaster / 45 min incline walk or treadmill)

### MONDAY – CARDIO

- StairMaster – Solid Zone 2 (or 40 min Elliptical / 45 min incline walk or treadmill)

### TUESDAY – STRENGTH (4 Rounds)

- KB Goblet Squats – 10 reps – 15 lbs
- DB Shoulder Press – 8 reps – 8 lbs
- KB Swings – 15 reps – 15 lbs
- DB Renegade Rows – 8 / side – 8 lbs
- Plank – 30 sec (drop to knees if needed)

### WEDNESDAY – WALK

- 30–45 Min Walk – Zone 1 to Low Zone 2 (a Bambi walk counts — easy, conversational pace)

### THURSDAY – UNILATERAL (20 Min EMOM, rotate each minute)

- Step-Ups – 8 / leg – 10 lbs
- KB Row – 8 / arm – 15 lbs
- DB Curl to Press – 8 reps – 5 lbs
- Russian Twists – 16 reps – 5 lbs (feet down to make it easier)

Start the next exercise at the top of each minute. 20 minutes = 5 full rotations.

### FRIDAY – CARDIO

- StairMaster – High Zone 2 (or 40 min Elliptical / 60 min incline walk or treadmill)

### SATURDAY – REST / MAKE-UP DAY

- Full day of physical rest, or make up a missed workout from earlier in the week (Sun–Fri).

### 🏋️ CARDIO MACHINES (Sun / Mon / Fri — toggle in the app)

- **Elliptical:** Resistance 4–8, smooth steady pace, stand tall with light hands.
- **StairMaster:** A steady level she can hold the whole time. Stand tall, light touch on the rails (don't lean). Minutes build with her Sunday phase: **Phase 1 = 20 min · Phase 2 = 30 min · Phase 3 = 40 min.**
- Defaults: Sunday = Elliptical, Monday & Friday = StairMaster (twice a week).

### 🐕 DAILY BAMBI WALKS (every day, including rest days)

- **Guidance:** 20–30 min each walk · **45 min a day** total. Easy pace is fine — let Bambi sniff. Want more out of it? Add a brisk 5–10 min stretch in the middle.
  - Lifting days (Sun / Tue / Thu): keep them easy — they help recovery.
  - Wednesday: one Bambi walk can be the 30–45 min Wednesday walk.
  - Saturday (rest): a longer, relaxed walk is a great way to stay active.
- Log **morning** and **afternoon** minutes (5-minute steps). The total shows "25 / 45 min" and turns green with ✅ at 45. Bonus activity — never affects streaks or day completion.
- Badges: **Bambi's Best Friend 🐾** (50 walks) · **Pack Leader 🐕** (200 walks) · **Daily Dose 🐶** (45 min on 30 days). 📊 chart of daily Bambi-walk minutes.

## 🔄 PROGRESSION (Rule of 4 · two sessions in a row at target to advance)

### Phase 1: The Foundation

- Sunday: Hit 3 complete rounds inside the 20-minute cap (checked automatically).
- Tuesday: Complete all 4 rounds with clean form at the starting reps and weights.
- Thursday: Complete the full 20-minute EMOM with clean form, finishing each set inside its minute.

### Phase 2: The Volume Builder (same weights, more reps)

- Sunday: Hit 4 complete rounds in 20 minutes. Deadlifts 14, Push-ups 10, Snatches 8 / arm, Thrusters 12.
- Tuesday: +2 reps on every move — Squats 12, Press 10, Swings 17, Rows 10 / side, Plank 40 sec.
- Thursday: +2 reps on every move — Step-Ups 10 / leg, Rows 10 / arm, Curl to Press 10, Twists 20.

### Phase 3: The Strength Step (heavier weights, back to starting reps)

- Sunday (one at a time):
  1. Use the 20 lb KB for Deadlifts only. Snatches stay at 15 lb.
  2. Move Snatches to the 20 lb KB once shoulders feel solid.
  3. Scale Thruster dumbbells from 8 lb toward 10 lb.
- Tuesday:
  - KB Goblet Squats / Swings: 15 lb → 20 lb KB.
  - DB Shoulder Press / Renegade Rows: 8 lb → 10 lb.
- Thursday:
  - Step-Ups: 10 lb → 12 lb.
  - KB Row: 15 lb → 20 lb KB.
  - DB Curl to Press: 5 lb → 8 lb.
  - Russian Twists: 5 lb → 8 lb.
