# 6-MONTH MASTER WORKOUT BLUEPRINT

Source of truth for the v2 app (`v2/index.html`). Finalized Oct 30, 2026.

## 🗓️ THE WEEKLY ROUTINE

### SUNDAY – THE BURNER (20 Min AMRAP)

- KB Deadlifts – 15 reps – 25 lbs
- Push-ups – 10 reps
- KB Snatches – 8 reps / arm – 25 lbs
- DB Devil's Press – 10 reps – 10 lbs
- Then: 45 Min Incline Walk – Zone 2 leading into Low Zone 3 (Let heart rate climb naturally into Zone 3 during the second half)

### MONDAY – ACTIVE RECOVERY WALK

- 45 Min Incline Walk – Solid Zone 2

### TUESDAY – STRENGTH (4 Rounds)

- KB Goblet Squats – 12 reps – 25 lbs (Include a strict 2-sec pause at the bottom)
- Two-Handed KB High Pulls – 12 reps – 25 lbs (Symmetric straight pull)
- DB Floor Press – 10 reps – 15 lbs (Use a 4-sec lowering phase + 1-sec dead stop at the bottom)
- KB Swings – 20 reps – 25 lbs
- Plank – 60 sec
- Rest 60 seconds between rounds. Keep it active with a light step-touch or boxer bounce.

### WEDNESDAY – RESTORATIVE RESET

- 20–30 Min Restorative / Yin Yoga Routine (Online Search)
- Focus: Passive floor stretches for hips, chest, and upper back. Avoid any classes with power movements, planks, or push-ups.

### THURSDAY – UNILATERAL & FLOW INTERVALS (25 Min Total)

- Minute 1: KB Reverse Lunge Pass-Through – 10 total lunges (5 / leg) – 25 lbs
- Minute 2: Single-Arm KB Rows – 10 reps / arm – 25 lbs
- Minute 3: DB Push Press – 10 reps – 12–15 lbs (Slight knee dip-and-drive)
- Minute 4: DB Plank Pull-Throughs – 12 total pulls – 10 lbs
- Minute 5: MANDATORY RESET – Full 60 Seconds of Complete Rest
- Repeat for 5 total rounds. Aim to complete minutes 1–4 within 45 seconds to keep a 15-second micro-rest.

### FRIDAY – REST / MAKE-UP DAY

- Full day of physical rest.

### SATURDAY – THE AEROBIC ENGINE (PRIMARY OPTION)

- 30–45 Min Continuous Beach Run (No Intervals) – Strict Zone 2 Target
- Execution: Run on wet, packed sand near the water line. Keep a steady, rhythmic jog where you can breathe entirely through your nose.

### SATURDAY – THE GYM BACKUP (WEATHER / LOGISTICS OPTION)

- 30–45 Min Continuous Pacing (No Intervals) – Strict Zone 2 Target
- Equipment Options:
  - Option A (Elliptical): Resistance level 6–10, low/flat incline, smooth gliding pace.
  - Option B (Stationary Bike): Resistance high enough to push firmly through heels, cadence 80+ RPM.

## 🔄 PHASE-BY-PHASE AUTOREGULATED PROGRESSION TRACK

**The Rule of 4:** You only advance weights or volume when you can hit your target rep counts across ALL rounds/active minutes with perfect form and required tempos.

### Phase 1: The Pacing Floor (Current Baseline)

- Sunday: Hit 4 complete rounds inside the 20-minute cap. Focus on minimizing transition times between exercises.
- Tuesday: Complete all target reps across all 4 rounds cleanly with the active 60-second rest window.
- Thursday: Survive all 25 minutes while strictly keeping your 15-second micro-rest buffer during active minutes 1–4.

### Phase 2: The Volume & Density Escalation

- Sunday: Expand your time block to a 25-minute cap. Advance once you can complete 5 full rounds within 25 minutes using the same starting weights.
- Tuesday: Keep weights constant. If feeling great, focus on clean rep speed or add 2 reps to your Goblet Squats and Floor Presses.
- Thursday: Increase the rep density inside each 60-second active window while keeping starting weights the same:
  - KB Reverse Lunges: Push to 12 total lunges (6 / leg)
  - Single-Arm KB Rows: Push to 12 reps / arm
  - DB Push Press: Push to 12 reps
  - DB Plank Pull-Throughs: Push to 16 total pulls

### Phase 3: The Strength Load Matrix

- Sunday: Drop back down to a strict 20-minute time cap to handle the heavy iron. Introduce the 35 lb kettlebell sequentially:
  1. Use 35 lbs for Sunday Deadlifts only. Snatches stay at 25 lbs.
  2. Move Snatches to the 35 lb KB once shoulder stability feels completely locked in.
  3. Scale up Devil's Press dumbbells from 10 lbs toward 12–15 lbs.
- Tuesday: Apply the Rule of 4 to step up your loads:
  - KB Goblet Squats / Swings / High Pulls: Move from the 25 lb KB to the 35 lb KB. Drop starting reps by 4 and slowly build back to target lines.
  - DB Floor Press: Advance from 15 lbs to 20 lbs.
- Thursday: Reset all rep targets back to Phase 1 baselines (10 reps / 12 pulls) and increase the load:
  - KB Rows: Upgrade to your 35 lb KB.
  - DB Push Press: Upgrade to 20 lbs.
  - DB Plank Pull-Throughs: Upgrade to 12–15 lbs.
  - KB Reverse Lunges: Upgrade to your 35 lb KB.

## 📈 REVISED BI-WEEKLY PROGRESSION TRACKER

- Week 2 (Tuesday): Complete Tuesday's circuit with zero extra rest or technical pauses.
- Week 4 (Sunday): Complete Sunday's 45 min incline walk without slowing down after lifting.
- Week 6 (Form Mastery): Add a 3-second negative lowering phase to every single lunge on Thursday.
- Week 8 (Tuesday Density): Transition your 60s Tuesday rest window from passive sitting into an active boxer bounce or slow step-touch.
- Week 10 (Sunday Load Step 1): Introduce the 35 lb kettlebell strictly for Sunday Deadlifts.
- Week 12 (Thursday Depth): Add a strict 2-second hold at the bottom of your reverse lunges.
- Week 14 (Sunday Phase 2 Check): Attempt to stretch Sunday's time block to 25 minutes to target that 5-round milestone.
- Week 16 (Metabolic Rucking): Add a 10 lb pack or weighted vest to Sunday and Saturday cardio walks to increase calorie burn without joint impact.
- Week 18 (Tuesday Tempo): Introduce a strict 3-second descent on every DB Floor Press on Tuesday.
- Week 20 (Unilateral Power): Shift Thursday's DB Push Press to a Single-Arm KB Clean and Press (5 reps / arm) using your 25 lb bell.
- Week 22 (Power Modification): Tuesday KB Swings ➔ Single-Arm Swings (10/arm) to challenge your unilateral grip power.
- Week 24 (The Final Test): Check your weight against your 180 lb target, and tackle Sunday's AMRAP using whatever heavy kettlebell combination your body has cleared you to use.
